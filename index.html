Bushy Athletic FC

Based in South Dublin, Bushy Athletic FC is a grassroots club built on friendship, community pride, and a genuine love for football. Competing in the United Churches Football League (UCFL), we pair strong camaraderie with a fierce competitive drive - a formula that has led to our 2022 Shield victory and lifting the 2026 League title.

  • 3A League Champions 2026 🏆
  • Shield Champs 2022 🛡
